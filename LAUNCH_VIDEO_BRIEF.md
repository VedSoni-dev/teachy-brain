# Launch Video Brief — Teachy

> For: `[VIDEO COMPANY NAME]`
> Purpose: Product launch video for Product Hunt, Hacker News, YC Demo Day, and social media
> Length: 60–90 seconds
> Style: Clean, dark, premium feel — think Apple keynote meets retro computing

---

## What we are

A free, open-source macOS menu bar AI tutor that sees your screen, points at real UI, and teaches you software hands-on. Not videos. Not docs. The AI watches you do it and corrects you in real-time.

## Brand identity

- **App name:** Teachy
- **Tagline:** "Free 1:1 teaching for everything — practice on the real screen, not another video player."
- **Website:** https://vedsoni-dev.github.io/teachy/
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/VedSoni-dev/teachy
- **License:** MIT (open source, free forever)
- **Target audience:** Students learning AI/software + companies onboarding employees

## Visual style

- **Color palette:** Deep black background (#000000), electric blue accent (#0A84FF), white text (#FFFFFF), subtle gray for secondary text (#8E8E93)
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif (SF Pro, Inter, or Helvetica Neue). Bold headers, light body text.
- **UI aesthetic:** macOS native — dark mode, rounded corners, menu bar icon, notch-style panel. The app feels like it belongs in the macOS status bar, not like a website crammed into a window.
- **Motion style:** Smooth spring animations (think boring.notch or Linear app). Subtle parallax. No flashy transitions. Cursor movements should feel like a real person is being guided — bezier arcs, not instant jumps.
- **Mood:** Confident, minimal, premium. The opposite of a noisy EdTech ad. More like an Apple product video than a Coursera ad.

## Video structure (60–90 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Problem (0:00–0:15)
Show a student staring at a YouTube tutorial. They nod along. Cut to them opening the real tool — frozen, confused, tabs everywhere. The gap between "watching" and "doing."

**Visuals:**
- Split screen: left side = polished tutorial video playing, right side = student's messy real screen
- The tutorial video fades to static/noise
- Text overlay: "Watching is not learning."

### Beat 2: The Product (0:15–0:45)
The app appears in the macOS menu bar — a small icon, unobtrusive. Click. The notch panel slides open with a spring animation. The learner picks a course. Push-to-talk activates. The AI captures the screen, coaches with spoken words, and POINTS at the exact UI element with a blue cursor overlay.

**Visuals:**
- macOS menu bar with the app icon (dark, minimal)
- Notch panel opening animation (spring, smooth)
- Course list with progress indicators
- Push-to-talk activation (ctrl+option indicator)
- Screen capture flash (subtle)
- **The key moment:** Blue cursor arc pointing at a real UI element on the screen. The learner's mouse follows the guide. They click. A green checkmark appears. The AI says "Nice — next step."
- Vision verification: a subtle scan animation over the completed step, then a green "Done" badge
- Teach-back: the AI asks "In your own words, what did you just do?" — learner responds, gets confirmation

### Beat 3: Scale & Vision (0:45–1:05)
Zoom out. Show multiple screens — students at different universities, different courses (GitHub, AI, coding). Then transition to enterprise: a dashboard showing team progress, skill gaps, onboarding metrics.

**Visuals:**
- Multiple MacBook screens showing different courses
- University campus backdrop (subtle, not stock-photo-ish)
- Enterprise dashboard: clean, dark, charts showing "47/50 engineers completed onboarding"
- Connector system: a visual of an API spec being parsed into a course
- Text overlay: "Open source. Free for students. Enterprise pays."

### Beat 4: Call to Action (1:05–1:15)
App icon centered on black. GitHub stars animating up. Download button. "Free forever."

**Visuals:**
- App icon (large, centered)
- GitHub URL + star count
- "Download free" button
- MIT license badge
- "Star us on GitHub" CTA
- Website URL

## Key product moments to show (pick 3–4)

1. **The cursor pointing** — the blue bezier arc animating from Teachy to a real UI element. This is the signature visual. Make it feel alive.
2. **The notch panel** — the macOS menu bar icon → spring-open panel animation. Should feel native to macOS.
3. **Vision verification** — the moment the AI scans the screen and confirms "you did it." Green checkmark or badge.
4. **Voice interaction** — push-to-talk waveform, the learner speaking, the AI responding with spoken coaching.
5. **Course progress** — moving from one step to the next, progress bar filling, sense of momentum.
6. **Enterprise dashboard** — clean, dark admin view with team metrics. Show it briefly but make it look premium.

## Audio

- **Music:** Ambient, minimal, electronic. Think Tycho or Bonobo — not corporate, not hype. Subtle piano or synth pads. Builds slightly during the product demo, drops for the CTA.
- **Voiceover:** Optional. If used, keep it conversational — not announcer-y. "This is [app name]. It watches your screen and teaches you software hands-on." That tone.
- **SFX:** Subtle UI sounds — a soft click when the menu bar icon is tapped, a gentle chime on completion, a quiet waveform hum during voice input. Nothing jarring.

## Do NOT

- No stock footage of "diverse team high-fiving"
- No upbeat ukulele music
- No "revolutionizing education" language
- No busy motion graphics or particle effects
- No corporate voiceover
- No pricing slides in the video (save that for the landing page)
- No "AI-powered" badge (everyone has that, it means nothing)
