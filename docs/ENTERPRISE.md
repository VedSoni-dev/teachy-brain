# Teachy Enterprise — Sales & Offer Brief

Internal / external one-pager for the company product. Website: [`academy/enterprise.html`](../academy/enterprise.html).

---

## One-liner

**Teachy Enterprise** = **bespoke AI for your company** + **Teachy’s 1:1 teaching software** so every employee becomes **AI fluent** on the tools and workflows you actually use.

---

## Problem

| Reality | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Orgs buy ChatGPT / Claude / Copilot / internal AI seats | Licenses rise; daily use stays shallow |
| “AI training” = LMS videos + webinars | No transfer to the real desktop |
| Completion certificates | No proof people can do the job with AI |
| Generic prompts & policies | Don’t match SOPs, risk, or role |

**Buyer pain quote (target):** *“We paid for AI. Nobody really knows how to use it on their work.”*

---

## Solution

### Layer 1 — Bespoke AI

- Workflow-specific assistants, prompts, and integrations  
- Role patterns (sales, ops, eng, support, leadership)  
- Guardrails aligned with company policy  

### Layer 2 — Teaching software (Teachy)

- 1:1 coach on the **real screen** (point → act → check → remember)  
- Custom curriculum on **your** tools and SOPs  
- Verification + teach-backs (skills, not watch time)  

Same engine as consumer **Teachy** (free personal product). Enterprise is custom AI + custom paths + rollout.

---

## Why now

**AI fluency is the new literacy.**  
Every company is buying tools; almost none are building practice systems. Competitors sell seats or content libraries. Teachy sells **practice + verification** on the real computer.

---

## Offer tiers

| Motion | Scope | Outcome |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Pilot** (~4 weeks, one team) | Discovery, 1–N custom paths, bespoke AI for the use case, coaching, proof pack | Leadership can see verified wins |
| **Rollout** | Multi-team paths, admin guidance, iteration | Company-wide fluency program |
| **Partner** | Deep bespoke AI + continuous curriculum | Strategic AI + teaching stack |

Pricing is **bespoke** (cohort size × path depth × AI build). Always start with a fixed-scope pilot.

---

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

- 50–5,000 employees (pilot with 10–100)  
- Already buying or rolling out mainstream AI tools  
- Has a champion in People / L&D / Ops / CTO org  
- Cares about **usage quality**, not just seat count  
- Willing to pilot one function before company-wide  

**Not a fit (yet):** pure compliance checkbox training with no practice appetite; Windows-only fleets until Mac loop is productized for them.

---

## Pilot process

1. **Discover** — roles, tools, SOPs, risk  
2. **Build** — bespoke AI + Teachy path(s) for cohort  
3. **Coach** — employees learn by doing; iterate on stuck points  
4. **Prove** — outcomes pack + rollout plan  

---

## Competitive frame

| Alternative | Gap |
|-------------|-----|
| LMS / LinkedIn Learning | Video ≠ skill on *your* UI |
| AI vendor “academy” | Generic; no verification on real work |
| Internal wiki + lunch & learn | No practice loop, no memory |
| Hire consultants for demos | Demo day ≠ daily fluency |
| **Teachy Enterprise** | Bespoke AI + act-first coaching + verified outcomes |

---

## Consumer vs Enterprise

| | **Teachy** | **Teachy Enterprise** |
|--|------------|------------------------|
| Who | Individuals | Companies |
| Price | Free | Bespoke |
| Curriculum | Open / public paths | Custom SOPs + roles |
| AI layer | Learner’s own tools | Bespoke systems for the org |
| Goal | Anyone can learn anything by doing | Whole workforce AI fluent |
